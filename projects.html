Functional Network Disruption as Biomarkers for Adolescent Concussion

Used graph theory-based modelling to observe functional connectivity in adolescent concussion, observed decreased efficiency, strength and increased clustering in individuals with concussion. Presented project at the Child and Youth Mental Health Research Day at the Hospital for Sick Children (SickKids), and the Tri-Campus Undergraduate Research Conference at the University of Toronto.
